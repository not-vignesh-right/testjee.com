# TestJEE — Overview

## What is TestJEE?

TestJEE is an **online mock test platform for JEE Main aspirants**. It simulates the real National Testing Agency (NTA) exam environment, giving students a realistic practice experience from the comfort of their own device.

Built for **Gyan-edge Testing Agency (GTA)**, TestJEE helps students:
- Practice with **full-length mock tests** that mirror the actual JEE Main paper
- Track their **scores and progress** over time
- Analyze **subject-wise performance** to identify strengths and weaknesses
- Build **exam temperament** by practicing under timed conditions

---

## How It Works

### 1. Sign Up & Login
Students create an account with their name, email, mobile number, and password. After email verification, they can log in anytime. Passwords can be reset via email if forgotten.

### 2. Student Dashboard
After logging in, students land on their personalized dashboard which shows:
- A **motivational quote** that changes every few seconds to keep spirits high
- **Key stats** — total exams taken, best score, average score, and accuracy
- A **score trend graph** showing improvement over time
- **Exam history** — every past attempt with score, questions attempted, and time spent
- A button to **start a new mock test**

### 3. Taking an Exam
The exam interface looks and feels exactly like the real NTA JEE Main portal:
- **75 questions** across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
- **3-hour timer** that starts on the server (can't be cheated by refreshing)
- **Question palette** showing which questions are answered, visited, or marked for review
- **Auto-save** — answers are saved instantly so nothing is lost if the browser crashes
- **Auto-submit** — the exam submits automatically when time runs out

### 4. Results & Analysis
After completing an exam, students get a detailed breakdown:
- **Overall summary** — total correct, wrong, unattempted, and time spent
- **Subject-wise cards** — accuracy and stats for Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics separately
- **Question-by-question analysis** — each question shows the student's answer, the correct answer, time spent, and whether it was correct, wrong, or skipped (color-coded for easy scanning)

### 5. Settings
Students can update their profile information:
- Name, mobile number, class (11th/12th/Dropper), school or coaching institute, and target exam year
- Change their password

## Key Highlights

- **Realistic Exam Simulation** — The interface matches NTA's actual portal, so students aren't surprised on exam day
- **Progress Tracking** — Dashboard charts and stats help students see their improvement over time
- **Secure & Fair** — Server-side timers prevent cheating; each student sees only their own data
- **Easy to Use** — Clean, modern design with simple navigation
- **Works on Any Device** — Responsive design optimized for desktop but usable on mobile/tablet
