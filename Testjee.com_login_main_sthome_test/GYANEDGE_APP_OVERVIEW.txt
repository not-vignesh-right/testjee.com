GYAN EDGE TESTING AGENCY (GTA) PLATFORM - COMPREHENSIVE SYSTEM OVERVIEW
=========================================================================

1. PRODUCT IDENTITY & VISION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Gyan Edge GTA interface is a premier, high-fidelity exam simulation platform designed to bridge the gap between preparation and performance. It is not just a mock test app; it is a full-fledged "Flight Simulator" for competitive exams. 

Developed as the technological arm of Gyan Edge, this platform ensures that students don't just learn concepts but master the art of test-taking in an environment that allows them to practice with the exact User Interface (UI) they will encounter on the actual exam day.

2. COMPREHENSIVE EXAM COVERAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike single-domain platforms, GTA is engineered to support a wide spectrum of competitive examinations across India. The system's flexible architecture adapts to different patterns, subjects, and marking schemes.

Supported Exam Verticals:
*   NO.1: JEE (Main & Advanced) - Engineering Entrance
*   NO.2: NEET (UG) - Medical Entrance
*   NO.3: NDA - National Defence Academy
*   NO.4: KCET - Karnataka Common Entrance Test
*   NO.5: Other State & Central Competitive Exams

3. CONTENT POWERHOUSE: PYQS & MOCK TESTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Content is the heart of the GTA platform. We provide an exhaustive repository ensuring students practice with the most relevant material.

A. Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
   - The platform is loaded with authentic past papers from all supported exams.
   - Shift-wise papers for JEE to cover every variation in difficulty.
   - Year-by-year archives for NEET, NDA, and KCET.
   - "Real-Exam Mode" for PYQs: Students can attempt a paper from 2023 exactly as it appeared then, not just as a PDF list.

B. Curated Mock Tests
   - High-quality predicted papers designed by Gyan Edge experts.
   - Difficulty levels matched to current trends.

4. "INCH-BY-INCH" EXAM INTERFACE DETAILS (NTA REPLICA)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The interface is a pixel-perfect clone of the official National Testing Agency (NTA) software. Every button, color, and behavior is replicated to eliminate exam-day anxiety.

A. The Exam Screen Layout
   - Header: Contains the Gyan Edge logo (top left) and the Candidate's Photo & Name (top right).
   - Timer: A high-precision digital countdown timer that changes color warnings as time runs out. It auto-submits the exam when it hits 00:00:00.
   - Question Area: Clear, readable question text with support for complex equations, diagrams, and images.

B. The Question Palette (Detailed Status Codes)
   The side panel provides a real-time "Bird's Eye View" of the student's progress using official color codes:
   [ White  ] : Not Visited - You have not reached this question yet.
   [ Red    ] : Not Answered - You viewed this but moved on without answering.
   [ Green  ] : Answered - You have saved an answer. (Will be evaluated)
   [ Purple ] : Marked for Review - You want to come back to this later. (Will NOT be evaluated)
   [ Purple+Green Dot ] : Answered & Marked for Review - You answered but marked it. (Will be evaluated)

C. Navigation & Control
   - "Save & Next": Saves response and moves forward.
   - "Clear Response": Instantly resets a selected option.
   - "Mark for Review & Next": Flags question for later.
   - Subject Switching: Tabs allow instant jumping between Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (or Biology/General Ability for other exams) at any time.

5. AUTHENTICATION & STUDENT EXPERIENCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
We prioritize ease of access and data security.

A. Frictionless "Magic Link" Login
   - No passwords to forget.
   - Students enter their registered Email ID.
   - A secure login link is sent instantly to their inbox.
   - One click grants access.

B. Student Profile Management
   - Auto-Profile Creation: First-time users are automatically registered in the database.
   - Session Persistence: If a student accidentally closes the tab or browser, their login state is preserved.
   - Name & Identity: The student's name is securely stored and displayed throughout the exam session for a personalized experience.

C. Deployment & Privacy
   - The system uses Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring strict data privacy.
   - A student can ONLY see their own results and history.
   - No cross-access of data between users.

6. RESULTS & ANALYTICS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The learning loop is closed with immediate, detailed feedback.

- Instant Scoring: Results are generated the millisecond the exam is submitted.
- Marking Scheme Adherence: 
    - Correct Answer: +4 Marks (Typical for JEE/NEET)
    - Incorrect Answer: -1 Mark (Negative marking enforced)
    - Unanswered: 0 Marks
- Detailed report showing attempted count, accuracy rate, and subject-wise breakdown.

7. TECHNICAL ROBUSTNESS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tech Stack: Built on Vue.js (for high performance) and Supabase (for real-time database reliability).
- Responsive Design: While optimized for Desktop (Mock Exam view), it remains fully functional on tablets and mobile devices for study on-the-go.
- Fast Loading: Optimized for low-bandwidth connections to reach students across all of India.

Summary:
The Gyan Edge GTA Platform is the definitive solution for serious aspirants. By combining the vast content library of PYQs for JEE, NEET, NDA, and KCET with a technically superior NTA-replica interface, we ensure that when a student walks into the exam hall, they feel right at home.
